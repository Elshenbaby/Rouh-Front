How it's counted
  • SUs are the people in Rouh who signed up in the term or days picked. Rouh may hold a few percent fewer than EXPA, because people who register straight on aiesec.org without Alpha may be missing.
  • APPs, ACC, APDs and REs follow those people to today (a cohort): someone who signed up in these days and was approved later counts here. EXPA's count of approvals made in these days, by anyone, is on each function's Performance view.
  • Sources come from "How did you hear about AIESEC?" and the campaign or campus the signup came through.
  • Contact rate, interviews, assigned and out of process come from what your LC records in Rouh.
  • Days can't start before 1 Feb 2026, when Rouh's records start.
