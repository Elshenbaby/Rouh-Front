iGTa analysis

The people who signed up in these days (iCX: who applied), and how far each has got by now: a cohort, by member and team. For what happened in these days, as EXPA counts it, see Performance.

How it's counted
  • The list: people who applied to our opportunities in the term picked at the top (or the days you choose). Each is followed to today: accepted, approved, realized, whenever that happened.
  • So it isn't EXPA's count for the term. Someone who signed up before the term and was approved in it is counted by EXPA (and on Performance) but isn't on this list. Someone on this list approved after the term is counted here, not by EXPA for the term.
  • Each person counts once, at the furthest step any of their applications reached. Applications counts every application they sent, at any time.
  • Contacted, assigned, teams and members come from what your LC records in Rouh. If nobody is assigned or marked contacted, those show 0.
  • From 1 Feb 2026: Rouh's records start that day, so the dates can't go earlier. For older days use Performance, which reads EXPA's analytics.
