iGTe analysis

CU · November (1 Nov – 8 Nov 2026). Every step counted on the day it happened, straight from EXPA.

How it's counted
  • The funnel, the LC table and the trend are EXPA's analytics for exactly the days picked, the same numbers LCs see in EXPA. Any days work, including past years.
  • Each step is counted on the day it happened, whoever it happened to: approved = applications approved in these days, even if the person signed up years ago. That's why it can differ from Pipeline & team, which follows the people who signed up in these days.
  • Signups (oGX) are people who registered in EXPA; opens (iCX) are opportunities opened. Applicants counts each person once; applications counts every application.
  • Approved leaves out approvals that were broken (EXPA removes the approval date). Broken approvals are shown on their own.
  • Finished is experiences that ended in these days; completed is those whose EP also finished the closing steps. Both are dated by the experience's end.
  • Breakdowns (countries, partner LCs, projects, members, campaigns) and step times aren't in EXPA's analytics, so they come from Rouh's own records, from 1 Feb 2026. The line above them says how much of EXPA's number they hold.
  • Days are EXPA's days (UTC), so a late-night approval in Cairo can fall on the next day, as in EXPA.
