How it's counted
  • Signups, applicants, accepted, approvals, realizations and completions are EXPA's analytics for the term picked, per LC: each counted on the day it happened, the same numbers LCs see in EXPA. Approvals that were broken don't count.
  • Completions are experiences that ended in the term and were completed.
  • Opens are the LC's opportunities open on EXPA now; raising, B2C campaigns, BD deals, members and retention come from Rouh; finance from the LC financial tools.
  • Scores put each LC against the best LC in the network on each metric, then group them into the OD clusters.
